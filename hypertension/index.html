Hypertension

Confirm it, decide when to treat, pick the drug and dose, and work up pressure that will not come down.

Trials

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. Built for adults seen in clinic, to US guidance. Not for children, pregnancy emergencies, or blood pressure in hospital.

The 2025 AHA/ACC guideline itself could not be read for this build: its thresholds, goals and class/level grades come from published papers that report it, and are marked "AHA/ACC 2025, as reported". Doses come from the FDA labels; rows marked expert practice or teaching are labeled. Formularies and single-pill combinations vary, so adjust to your institution. Diagrams are schematic.

Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored, and there are no analytics. Do not enter names, record numbers, or dates of birth.
