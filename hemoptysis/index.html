Is it the lung?

Coughed up, bright red and frothy points to the lung; coffee-ground, acidic or mixed with food points to the stomach.

Airway, oxygen and pressure

A teaspoon is about 5 mL, a tablespoon about 15 mL. Estimates are rough; the airway and oxygen matter more.

6 · Trials

The evidence behind the practice

There is no randomized trial in massive hemoptysis. The tranexamic acid trials are small and excluded unstable patients; the embolization data are case series.

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. There is no US society guideline on massive hemoptysis. Nearly every step here is expert practice from critical care and interventional reviews, and is labelled so; imaging and embolization follow the ACR Appropriateness Criteria. Tranexamic acid has been studied only in non-massive hemoptysis, so no dose is given for massive bleeding; trial regimens appear on the trial cards as the trials gave them. Reversal doses are in the reversal tool and are not restated. Who can do bronchoscopy, embolization and thoracic surgery at night varies: adjust to your institution.
