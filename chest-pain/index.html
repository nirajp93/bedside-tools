Algorithm cutoffs · ng/L

The numbers this tool uses

Rule out: first value below very low (one draw, onset 3 h or more, normal ECG), or below low with a change under no change. Rule in: first value at or above high, or a change at or above rise. Anything else: observe. Changes are absolute, up or down.

99th percentile and reporting limit

When is a value "high"?

From each assay's FDA clearance (lithium-heparin plasma for the Siemens and Beckman values, K2-EDTA plasma for Abbott). A value above the sex-specific 99th percentile is myocardial injury; the algorithm cutoffs above are for triage only, not for diagnosis.

Other assays and point-of-care tests

Abbott Alinity, QuidelOrtho VITROS, Siemens Dimension and point-of-care devices are not offered: no guideline table found gives cutoffs for a full 0/1-hour pathway with them, or (VITROS) the guideline's very-low cutoff sits below the US reporting limit. Algorithm changes cannot be carried from one assay to another. Use the pathway your laboratory has validated, and adjust to your institution if its cutoffs differ from these.

7 · Trials

The studies behind the pathway

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. For adults with acute chest pain or other symptoms of a possible acute coronary syndrome. It does not apply to ST-elevation or occlusion MI, cardiac arrest or shock (see EKG emergencies). A rule-out excludes MI, not pulmonary embolism, aortic dissection or other causes. Cutoffs are assay-specific and come from published algorithms; laboratories validate their own pathways, so adjust to your institution. Evidence tiers are shown as class of recommendation and level of evidence (ACC/AHA), class and level (ESC), or "expert consensus" (ACC decision pathway); "expert practice" marks choices made for this tool.
