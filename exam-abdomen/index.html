Evidence tier: systematic reviews of diagnostic accuracy studies, except splenomegaly (one small study). Some figures within reviews come from ranges (pain migration in children). Exam technique, ascites manoeuvres, peritoneal and special signs, and the pain map: standard physical diagnosis teaching (expert practice); accuracy figures for these are not shown because they have not been verified against their primary studies. Combining likelihood ratios multiplies them, which assumes the findings are independent; overlapping findings use only the strongest one. Pre-test probabilities are yours to set; starting values are examples or the source's stated prevalence.
