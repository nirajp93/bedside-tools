For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. Targets follow the 2025 AHA/ACC high blood pressure guideline, with the 2026 AHA/ASA ischemic stroke, 2022 AHA/ASA intracerebral hemorrhage, 2023 AHA/ASA subarachnoid hemorrhage and 2022 ACC/AHA aortic disease guidelines for their conditions; the 2019 ESC Council on Hypertension position paper is shown where US guidance gives no number. Drug doses are from the current US prescribing information; where the 2025 guideline's dose table differs, both are shown. Esmolol, phentolamine and some uses of the others are outside their labelled indications. Evidence tiers are the AHA class and level (for example 1, C-LD), or "consensus" and "expert practice" where no graded guideline makes the statement. Pregnancy is shown only as a pointer: follow obstetric guidance. Drips on formulary, premixed concentrations and peripheral-line rules vary, so adjust to your institution.
