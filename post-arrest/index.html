6 · Trials

The trials behind the practice

Large randomized trials exist for temperature, the cath lab call, blood pressure, oxygen and CO2. Prognosis rests on observational studies.

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. Recommendations follow the 2025 American Heart Association guideline (Part 11) for US practice, with the 2025 European Resuscitation Council and European Society of Intensive Care Medicine guideline shown where it differs, and the 2023 Neurocritical Care Society guideline for prognosis. Evidence tiers are shown as the AHA class and level of evidence (for example 1, B-R), the ERC-ESICM or NCS strength, or "expert practice" where no guideline makes the statement. This tool gives no drug doses; seizure drugs, vasopressors and sedation link to the tools that hold them. Temperature devices, EEG and SSEP availability and cath lab pathways vary, so adjust to your institution. Decisions about withdrawing life support are made by the treating team with the family, never by this tool.
