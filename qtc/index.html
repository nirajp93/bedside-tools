1 · What it measures

The QT is the time the ventricles take to recharge

Each ventricular cell fires, holds a plateau, then repolarizes. The QT spans that whole cycle. Tap what a QT drug changes.

2 · Rate correction

Why Bazett overcorrects at fast rates

The QT shortens as the rate rises, so it is corrected to what it would be at 60 a minute. Drag the rate: the three formulas agree near 60 and part ways at the extremes.

3 · Measuring

Where the T wave ends

Most QT errors are measurement errors. Tap a situation.

4 · Repolarization reserve

Risk factors stack

One drug rarely causes torsades alone. Tap the factors present (Tisdale score, cardiac care unit patients).

5 · Narrowing

Why is the QT long?

Send potassium, magnesium and calcium and read the medicine list first; repeat the 12-lead. Only if it stays long off drugs: thyroid, ischemia, family history and a cardiology referral. Tap what you found: causes that no longer fit fade, each with the reason.

6 · Torsades

How torsades starts

A pause lengthens the next QT; an afterdepolarization lands on that long T wave and sets off a twisting polymorphic VT.

7 · Evidence

The studies behind the numbers

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. For adults. The drug list is a teaching subset, not a complete or curated database; a drug not listed is not known to be safe. Machine QT readings are often wrong: measure by hand before acting on a value. Polymorphic VT with a long QT is an emergency: see Code cards and Arrhythmias.
