What does not work

    1 · First question

    Major or not, and does it need reversal?

    A bleed is major when any one of three things is true. Tap one.

    Reversal agents are for bleeds at a critical site, bleeds that threaten life, and major bleeds that the first measures do not control. Everything else is treated by stopping the drug and fixing the source.

    6 · Labs

    A normal test can rule a drug out

    A normal thrombin time rules out dabigatran. A normal PT does not rule out apixaban, rivaroxaban or edoxaban. The INR measures warfarin, not the newer drugs.
