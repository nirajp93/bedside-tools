For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. "Reliable" means active against typical isolates and a standard choice in guidelines or labelling; "variable" means only some isolates, or one setting such as urine or skin. Neither replaces susceptibility testing. Local resistance, formulary and infectious-disease advice take priority.
