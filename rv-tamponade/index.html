6 · Evidence

How good is the bedside, and what the RV care rests on

Exam accuracy comes from small studies. Care of the failing right ventricle rests on physiology and expert consensus: no randomized trial has tested a fluid strategy, an inotrope or a support device for isolated right ventricular failure.

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. Tamponade is a clinical diagnosis: this tool sorts what you enter, it does not diagnose. Pressor and inotrope doses are on the Pressors and inotropes tool and are not restated here. Evidence tiers are the ESC class and level (I to III; A to C) or "expert practice" for rows that no graded guideline covers; consensus and scientific statements are named as such. Who drains (cardiology, interventional radiology, cardiac surgery) and who runs mechanical support vary: adjust to your institution.
