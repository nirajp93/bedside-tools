What has been tested, and what helped

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. A primer with no patient data entered. Example wording is expert practice, offered as a starting point to put in your own words. Who may act as surrogate, out-of-hospital orders, organ donation referral, medical examiner cases, brain death accommodation, and disputes over potentially inappropriate treatment are set by state law and hospital policy: adjust to your state and institution, and involve palliative care, ethics, chaplaincy and social work. No symptom dose appears here: doses live on the Opioids and Sedation tools. Evidence tiers are shown as each source grades them (strong or conditional with certainty for SCCM); policy statements, consensus statements and regulations are labelled as such.
