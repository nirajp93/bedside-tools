6 · Trials

The trials behind the practice

Almost no randomized trial enrolled patients in shock. Most catheter evidence uses surrogate endpoints or no control group, and most trials were industry funded.

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. This tool starts where VTE treatment stops: categories C3, D and E of the 2026 AHA/ACC classification. Categories, reperfusion grades and doses match the VTE treatment and code cards tools. Evidence tiers are the AHA/ACC class and level (1, 2a, 2b, 3: No Benefit, 3: Harm; A, B-R, B-NR, C-LD, C-EO) or the ESC 2019 class and level (I to III; A to C); rows marked expert practice are not from a graded guideline. The bleeding plan after lysis is adapted from the 2026 AHA/ASA stroke guideline, which was written for stroke, not PE. Who has a catheter team, cardiac surgery or ECMO, and the heparin protocol, vary: adjust to your institution.
