Built to the ATS/CHEST 2017 liberation guideline, the AARC 2024 guideline, the ERS/ATS 2017 noninvasive ventilation guideline and the ERS 2022 high-flow guideline, with the UK National Tracheostomy Safety Project algorithm and the FICM/ICS 2020 tracheostomy care guidance for the tracheostomy steps. The daily screen, first trial and cuff leak test are in the ventilator tool; sedation in the sedation tool. Airway teams, tracheostomy equipment and protocols vary, so adjust to your institution. Evidence tiers are shown as strength and certainty (strong or conditional; high, moderate, low or very low); rows marked expert practice are not from a graded guideline.
