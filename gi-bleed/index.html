Upper or lower

Findings that point upstream

Likelihood ratios for an upper source (Srygley 2012, systematic review). Above 1 favors upper; below 1 favors lower.

The ligament of Treitz divides upper from lower. A brisk upper bleed can come out red: hematochezia with instability is upper until shown otherwise (ACG 2023, key concept).
