Starting a heparin infusion

80 units/kg bolus, then 18 units/kg/h

Why: Weight-based dosing reaches a therapeutic aPTT sooner than a fixed 5,000-unit bolus and 1,000 units/h.

More

In the trial behind this nomogram, 97% of weight-dosed patients passed the therapeutic threshold within 24 hours against 77% with standard dosing, and recurrent clots were fewer.

Then titrate to your hospital's aPTT or anti-Xa nomogram; bolus and rate caps vary, so adjust to your institution. The heparin label itself gives fixed doses (5,000 units, then 20,000 to 40,000 units per 24 hours) and an aPTT of 1.5 to 2 times normal.

For pulmonary embolism that needs a parenteral start, the 2026 AHA/ACC guideline recommends LMWH over heparin (1, B-R), and finds LMWH reasonable even when a catheter procedure is likely (2a, C-LD).

Why: Apixaban and rivaroxaban were tested as single drugs with a higher starting dose; dabigatran and edoxaban were tested only after a heparin lead-in, so that is how they are licensed.

More

With warfarin, continue the injectable for at least 5 days and until the INR is in range (enoxaparin and fondaparinux labels; the warfarin label says 4 to 5 days of overlap).

Enoxaparin's labelled outpatient regimen is 1 mg/kg every 12 hours, for DVT without PE. The 1.5 mg/kg daily regimen is labelled for inpatients.

Reduced doses after 6 months are for preventing recurrence in patients who continue; see the duration card on the Treat tab.

7 · Exceptions

When a DOAC is the wrong drug

Antiphospholipid syndrome, pregnancy and breastfeeding, Child-Pugh C liver disease, a strong enzyme inducer, and the first weeks after bariatric surgery. Kidney failure and a few interactions change the dose or the choice among them.
