More: reference ranges

The tool uses TSH 0.4–4.0 mIU/L, free T4 0.8–1.8 ng/dL and free T3 2.3–4.2 pg/mL. Adjust to your lab. In pregnancy, without a local trimester range, TSH 0.1–4.0 is used for the first and second trimesters (ATA 2026, conditional, low).
