What to send, and when

First: CBC with indices, reticulocytes, smear, ferritin with iron and TIBC, creatinine. Draw iron, B12, folate and hemolysis labs before a transfusion when that does not delay care.

After a result: B12 and folate when macrocytic, or normocytic and unexplained. MMA when B12 is 180 to 350. LDH, haptoglobin and bilirubin when the index is 2 or more without bleeding, or with jaundice or dark urine. DAT when that panel is positive. TSH, protein electrophoresis and free light chains when normocytic and unexplained. Hemoglobin electrophoresis when the Mentzer index is under 13 and iron is replete.
