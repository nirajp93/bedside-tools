7 · Trials and cohorts

The evidence

Acute liver failure is rare; there are few randomized trials. Most recommendations rest on cohorts and expert opinion, and are labelled so.

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. Follows the 2023 American College of Gastroenterology acute liver failure guideline, with the 2017 EASL guideline where ACG gives no number. Evidence tiers are ACG GRADE (strong or conditional, with quality of evidence), ACG "Key Concept" (expert opinion, not graded) or EASL (evidence level, grade of recommendation 1 strong or 2 weak); "expert practice" where neither guideline makes the statement. Acetylcysteine doses are from the current US prescribing information (Acetadote); the non-acetaminophen regimen is the one used in the trial. Antiviral, acyclovir and other drug doses are not shown: dose them with pharmacy for kidney function. Transfer criteria, transplant listing and plasma exchange depend on the transplant center: adjust to your institution and call early.
