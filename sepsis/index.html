        1 · Words

        Sepsis is organ dysfunction; shock is a subset

        Screening: the 2026 guideline recommends NEWS, NEWS2, MEWS or SIRS over qSOFA as a single screening tool (strong, moderate). qSOFA (2 or more of: breathing 22 or more, any confusion, systolic 100 or less) flags risk of a poor outcome; it is not a definition, and not meeting it should not delay treatment.
