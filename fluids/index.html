Built to the Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2026 guideline and the European Society of Intensive Care Medicine fluid therapy guideline (parts 1 to 3, 2024 to 2025). Fluid compositions are from the US prescribing information. Vasopressor, diuretic, albumin-for-cirrhosis and DKA doses live in their own tools. Fluid products, monitors and protocols vary, so adjust to your institution. Evidence tiers are shown as strength and certainty (strong or conditional; high, moderate, low or very low), best practice statement, or expert practice for rows no graded guideline covers. Test cut-offs are shown with the population they were validated in.
