Why: Statin intensity is defined by the expected LDL drop, so the drop you see at 4–12 weeks tells you whether the dose and adherence are right.

More

Recheck: lipid panel 4–12 weeks after starting or changing, then every 6–12 months (class 1, A). Routine CK or liver tests without symptoms are not useful (class 3).

Interactions: simvastatin no more than 20 mg with amiodarone, amlodipine, ranolazine or bempedoic acid; no more than 10 mg with diltiazem, verapamil or dronedarone; no more than 40 mg with ticagrelor. Pravastatin no more than 40 mg with bempedoic acid. Never gemfibrozil with any statin. Colchicine: watch for muscle toxicity.

Other cautions: bempedoic acid raises urate and creatinine (gout 3.1% vs 2.1% in CLEAR). Bile acid sequestrants raise TG; avoid if TG ≥300. Omega-3 ethyl esters (with DHA) can raise LDL-C. Icosapent ethyl: more atrial fibrillation and bleeding in REDUCE-IT.

HoFH and FCS only (specialist): evinacumab 15 mg/kg IV every 4 weeks, lomitapide, olezarsen 80 mg monthly.

Send first

  • Lipid panel, nonfasting is fine; ask for Martin/Hopkins or Sampson/NIH LDL and non-HDL (class 1, B-NR). Fasting if nonfasting TG ≥400.
  • Lp(a), once in every adult, in nmol/L by an isoform-insensitive assay (class 1, B-NR).
  • A1C, TSH, CMP (eGFR, liver), UACR, and pregnancy test when relevant: secondary causes before calling it genetic (class 1, B-NR).

Why: One Lp(a) is enough because it is about 90% inherited and barely changes over life.

Send only after a result

  • apoB: on therapy at LDL and non-HDL goal with TG ≥150, diabetes or CKM (2a); to refine risk off therapy (2b).
  • hsCRP twice: borderline risk, to decide on a statin (2a).
  • CAC score: intermediate or select borderline risk when the decision is unclear; men ≥40, women ≥45 (class 1, B-R).
  • FH gene panel: probable FH (class 1); LDL ≥190 without a secondary cause (2a); LDL 160–189 (2b). Then cascade-test relatives.
  • CK: only with severe muscle symptoms or weakness.

Not recommended: NMR or particle-size panels to start therapy (class 3, no benefit).
