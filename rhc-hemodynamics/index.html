For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. Adults only. Pulmonary hypertension definitions are from the 2022 ESC/ERS guideline and the 7th World Symposium (2024), written for stable patients at rest. Shock thresholds are the 2026 SCAI SHOCK update (read in its public-comment draft) and the 2025 ACC concise guidance, and match the heart failure tool's Advanced tab. Cardiac power output, PAPi and RAP/wedge cut-offs come from single cohorts and predict outcome only modestly; they are shown as context, not as decision thresholds. Evidence tiers: ESC/ERS class and level; AHA/ACC class and level; expert consensus; cohort study; expert practice. Body surface area uses Du Bois unless the report's own value is entered; labs differ.

Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored, and there are no analytics. Do not enter names, record numbers, or dates of birth.
