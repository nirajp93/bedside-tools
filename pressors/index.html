    4 · Vasopressin

    Why vasopressin is added, not titrated

    It works on a different receptor from the catecholamines, so it adds pressure without more adrenergic drive. Tap a dose.

    6 · Inodilators

    Why milrinone and dobutamine can drop the pressure

    Both raise output and relax vessels. If output cannot rise enough, pressure falls.

Rows marked expert practice are not from a graded guideline. Evidence tiers are shown as strength and certainty (Surviving Sepsis Campaign, trauma and spinal cord guidelines), class and level (AHA/ACC), expert consensus (ACC, SCAI) or FDA label.
