Benzodiazepine sample regimens

Examples from the ASAM 2020 guideline (Appendix V and glossary) and US labels. Adjust to your institution's protocol.

ApproachRegimen
Symptom-triggeredChlordiazepoxide 25–100 mg PO when CIWA-Ar is 10 or more (mild 25–50, moderate 50–100, severe 75–100); reassess every 1–4 h for 24 h. ASAM 2020
Symptom-triggered, short-actingOxazepam 15 mg PO for CIWA-Ar 8–15, 30 mg for over 15. ASAM 2020 glossary
Front-loadingDiazepam 20 mg PO every hour until CIWA-Ar under 10; or diazepam 20 mg PO every 2 h for 3 doses; or chlordiazepoxide 50–100 mg PO every 1–2 h until CIWA-Ar under 10. ASAM 2020
Fixed scheduleChlordiazepoxide 25–100 mg every 4–6 h on day 1, every 6–8 h on day 2, every 8–12 h on day 3, at bedtime on day 4 (optional day 5), lowering the daily total by 25–50% each day. ASAM 2020
IV diazepam (label)10 mg IM or IV, then 5–10 mg in 3–4 h if needed. FDA label
LorazepamNeither the ASAM guideline nor the US label gives a withdrawal dose. Use your institution's protocol. Not sourced here

The chlordiazepoxide label maximum is 300 mg a day; front-loading can exceed it and belongs in a monitored setting. Labels advise lower doses in older or debilitated patients.

Phenobarbital: regimens that have been used

Only for clinicians experienced with it; IV phenobarbital only in highly supervised settings (ICU, CCU). Barbiturates are not preferred over benzodiazepines. ASAM 2020

  • Single dose with a benzodiazepine protocol: 10 mg/kg IV over 30 min. Rosenson 2013 trial; ASAM Appendix V
  • ICU adjunct once the protocol's maximum diazepam step (120 mg) has been reached: 60, then 120, then 240 mg IV every 30 min to RASS 0 to −2. ASAM Appendix V sample
  • ICU monotherapy: 130 mg IV every 30 min to RASS 0 to −1. ASAM Appendix V sample

Phenobarbital on top of large benzodiazepine doses adds respiratory depression: continuous monitoring, airway plan ready. The US injection is an unapproved product with no withdrawal indication.

Thiamine

Give thiamine before any carbohydrate in an at-risk patient. EFNS 2010

Prevention: 100 mg IV or IM daily for 3–5 days. ASAM 2020

Suspected Wernicke (2 of 4: dietary deficiency, eye signs, cerebellar signs, altered mental state or mild memory impairment): 200 mg three times daily, preferably IV EFNS 2010; ASAM's ICU discussion gives 200–500 mg IV every 8 h; ASAM 2020; keep it parenteral for at least 5 days. NICE CG100

Timeline after the last drink

ASAM 2020 section II.A. Delirium tremens timing differs within the guideline (another section says 24–48 h); treat the hours as typical, not fixed. Delirium lasting more than 72 h should prompt a check for drug-induced (benzodiazepine) delirium and other causes. ASAM 2020

What not to use

  • Beta-blockers to prevent or treat withdrawal seizures; they can mask signs and lower the seizure threshold. ASAM 2020
  • Oral or IV alcohol. ASAM 2020
  • Baclofen for withdrawal. ASAM 2020
  • Phenytoin for withdrawal seizures. ASAM 2020
  • Antipsychotics alone; only as an add-on for hallucinations or agitation not controlled by benzodiazepines, after a QTc check (QT tool). ASAM 2020
  • Dexmedetomidine or propofol as the treatment: dexmedetomidine is an add-on only and does not treat seizures; propofol only in patients already on a ventilator. ASAM 2020
  • Valproate in liver disease or in women who could become pregnant. ASAM 2020
  • Magnesium as a withdrawal treatment (give it only for a low magnesium). ASAM 2020

At discharge: treat the alcohol use disorder

Naltrexone 50 mg PO once daily: not with opioids, acute hepatitis or liver failure. FDA label; APA 2018, 1B

Acamprosate 666 mg PO three times daily; 333 mg three times daily when creatinine clearance is 30–50; contraindicated at 30 or less. Start once withdrawal is over. FDA label; APA 2018, 1B

Topiramate or gabapentin if those are not tolerated or preferred. APA 2018, 2C

5 · Workup that narrows

Is it withdrawal, or something else?

Send first: glucose, electrolytes with magnesium and phosphate, liver tests, blood count, blood alcohol, urine drug screen. Only if the picture does not fit: head CT (fall, focal signs, first or focal seizure), lumbar puncture (fever with confusion), ammonia, TSH, a toxicology consult. Tap a result.
