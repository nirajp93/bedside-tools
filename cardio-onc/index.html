Where things stand

1 · Baseline risk

Risk is counted before the first dose

HFA-ICOS sorts factors into very high, high and medium (1 or 2 points). One very-high or high factor sets the level; medium points add up. Tap a level.

2 · Mechanisms

Each drug injures a different part

Muscle, arteries, rhythm or immune attack: what you check follows from what the drug does. Tap a drug.

3 · Grading

CTRCD: mild, moderate, severe

Baseline LVEF 60%. Drag the LVEF now, and add a strain fall or a biomarker rise. The grade comes from the same rules as the Results tab.

4 · Strain

Strain falls before the ejection fraction

Global longitudinal strain is negative: about -20% is normal, and closer to zero is worse. What counts is the relative fall from the patient's own baseline. Baseline -20%: drag the value now.

5 · Cumulative dose

Anthracycline injury adds up

Convert any anthracycline to doxorubicin equivalents, then see where it sits on the dose ladder.

6 · Narrowing

New troponin rise on a checkpoint inhibitor

Send an ECG, troponin and an echocardiogram first; cardiac MRI next; biopsy only if still unsure. Tap what you found: causes that no longer fit fade, each with the reason.

7 · Trials

The studies behind the advice

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. For adults with cancer. Plans are made with the oncology team; any decision to hold or stop cancer treatment is shared with them. The risk tool covers anthracyclines, HER2-targeted therapy and VEGF inhibitors only. Other regimens (BCR-ABL inhibitors, myeloma therapy, RAF and MEK inhibitors, hormone therapy, CAR-T) are not covered here. Adjust to your institution's cardio-oncology pathway.
