4 · Bleeding risk

Three factors outweigh the rest

An active stomach or duodenal ulcer, bleeding in the 3 months before admission, and platelets below 50 each carried an odds ratio over 3 for bleeding in hospital. CHEST counts any one of them, or several lesser factors together, as too high a risk for an anticoagulant.

The IMPROVE bleeding score adds them up; 7 or more marked the 10% of patients who had over half the major bleeds. It was never tested as a rule for withholding prophylaxis, so this tool shows it as a caution, not a stop.

The trials behind the practice

For education only. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. For adults admitted with a medical illness; surgical, trauma, obstetric and pediatric patients need their own guidance.
